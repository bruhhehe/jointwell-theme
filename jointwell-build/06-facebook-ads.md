# 06. Facebook ads

## Campaign structure

One campaign, one ad set, many ads. Let Meta find the woman; do not slice the audience.

- Campaign: Sales objective, Advantage+ off, budget at ad set level. Name `JW_Prospecting_UK_W55-70`.
- Ad set: Women, 55 to 70, United Kingdom, no interests, no lookalikes. Placements: Facebook feed and Facebook Reels only (untick Instagram, Audience Network, Messenger). Optimise for Purchase. Budget £30 a day for the first week, £50 a day for weeks two and three. Attribution 7-day click, 1-day view.
- Ads: the angles below, one ad per angle, all in the same ad set so Meta allocates spend. Four live at a time in the first test (see testing plan).
- Pixel and conversions API through the Shopify Facebook and Instagram app; verify Purchase fires on the thank-you page before spending.

## Rules for every ad

Text she can read without her glasses: overlays at 60px or larger on 1080px canvases, one idea per ad, real homes, daylight, no models under 50, no music-video editing, slow cuts, subtitles on every video, no "clinically proven", no "relieves pain", no urgency that is not real, no "selling out". Every claim in an ad appears somewhere on the page it links to. Landing page for every ad is the homepage with the matching doorway, using the UTM below.

Primary text limits: about 125 characters visible before "See more". Put the whole idea in the first line.

## Format sizes

Image: 1:1 (1080 x 1080) for feed, 4:5 (1080 x 1350) for feed mobile. Video: 4:5 for feed, 9:16 for Reels; export the 9:16 with the subtitles inside the safe area (top 250px and bottom 350px clear).

## UTM and naming

Landing URL: `https://velagoods.co.uk/?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=paid&utm_campaign=jw_prospecting&utm_content={angle}_{format}_{variant}#{doorway}`

Ad name: `JW_{angle number}_{angle}_{IMG|VID}_{1:1|4:5|9:16}_{v1|v2|v3}`. Example: `JW_03_heattest_VID_4:5_v1`. Keep the same name in Meta and in the spreadsheet.

## Testing plan

**Week 1, £30 a day.** Four ads live in one ad set: Angle 1 image v1, Angle 3 video 30s, Angle 5 image v1, Angle 10 image v1. Nothing else.

**Kill rule.** After an ad has 20 purchases, if its cost per purchase is above £30, pause it. Before 20 purchases, do not touch anything; the numbers are noise.

**Swap rule.** When an ad is paused, replace it with the next variant of the same angle if the angle's best ad was under £35, or with the next angle in this order if not: 4, 7, 2, 6, 9a, 8 (only when reviews are real), 9b, 9c, 9e, 9d.

**Scale rule.** When an ad is under £25 cost per purchase after 30 purchases, raise the ad set budget by 20% every three days while it stays under £30. Stop raising when it crosses £30 for two consecutive days.

**Weeks 2 and 3, £50 a day.** Same rules. By the end of week 3 you should have two angles under £25 and know which format (image or video) the audience prefers.

**What to record, weekly, in a spreadsheet:** ad name, spend, purchases, cost per purchase, average order value, click-through rate, and the landing page conversion rate from Shopify for the UTM. Targets from the report: cost per purchase under £25, landing page conversion 2.5 to 4%, average order value £70.

**Before spending a pound:** the three placeholder reviews on the site are replaced with real ones or removed, "clinically proven" is gone (it is), the refund policy says 90 days, and the pixel fires on a test purchase.
