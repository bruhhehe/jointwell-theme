## What to do if a number is wrong

- Conversion under 2% on paid traffic: check the reviews are visible in the first screen on a phone, check the price and button are above the fold, read the ad and the page together for a mismatch, and check delivery times against reality before touching anything else.
- Cost per purchase over £35 on every angle: stop spending. The page, not the ads, is the problem. Get to 50 reviews and reshoot before restarting.
- AOV under £60: the two-wrap tier is not being chosen. Check it is preselected and the savings line is showing.
- Refunds over 8%: read every return reason. If it is "did not help", the expectations copy is too warm; if it is "did not fit", the size answer needs the diagram higher; if it is "took too long", delivery times on the page are wrong.
- Reviews under 20 at day 60: flow C is not firing on delivery. Check the trigger, then send a one-off to every customer.
