# 08. Content shot list: one day

Everything to photograph and film in a single day, in one real house with one garden, on a phone. Two women aged 55 to 70 (a customer or a relative who has agreed), plus grandad if he agrees, plus one grandchild whose face is never shown. No shots of the owner.

Kit: a recent phone, a £15 phone tripod, a kitchen thermometer with a probe, a hot water bottle, a wheat bag, the wrap fully charged (two wraps if possible), the box and its contents, a returns label printed, a mug, a newspaper, a jigsaw, a crossword, wellies and secateurs, a dressing gown, a cardigan. Daylight only; shoot between 9am and 3pm; turn the kitchen lights off.

Rules: real rooms as they are, no tidying beyond safety, no studio white, no filters, phone held level, subject not asked to smile at the camera. Shoot each still in portrait 4:5 and square 1:1. Film each video clip in 4:5 and again in 9:16, 10 to 20 seconds longer than needed, at 30 fps, with the phone on the tripod.

Naming: `JW_{number}_{short}_{4x5|1x1|9x16}.jpg/mp4`.
